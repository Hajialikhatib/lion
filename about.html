About Lion Hub Solution

A Modern Multi-Service Enterprise

Lion Hub Solution Company Limited is a premier modern multi-service corporate vendor based in Zanzibar, providing top-tier professional solutions across Tanzania and East Africa.


By leveraging highly skilled professionals, creative innovation, and cutting-edge technology, we execute every project with precision, integrity, and absolute customer satisfaction.

Our Identity

Vision: To be the most trusted and leading service provider in East Africa across electrical works, corporate supply, cleaning, creative services, and building finishing.

Mission: To deliver exceptional quality services driven by professionalism, accountability, integrity, and a customer-first approach.

Our Core Divisions

1. Electrical Installation

Comprehensive electrical system setup for residential, commercial, hotel, and industrial spaces. Services include wiring, routine maintenance, solar system installations, and technical troubleshooting.

2. General Supplies

Reliable supply and distribution of premium office stationery, books, administrative items, and educational materials tailored for government institutions and private enterprises.

3. Creative, Arts & Entertainment

Full-scale event management, corporate branding, media support, entertainment services, high-end sound system setups, and professional stage production for events of all scales.

4. General Cleaning Services

Modern and standardized sanitation and deep cleaning solutions for commercial complexes, corporate offices, hotels, residential properties, and institutional spaces.

5. Building Completion & Finishing

End-to-end interior and exterior space transformation, including professional painting, gypsum ceilings, precision tile fitting, renovations, and architectural finishing work.

Target Client Spectrum

We take pride in serving diverse sectors including Government Bodies, Private Corporations, Educational Institutions, Enterprises, Small/Large Businesses, and Private Clients.
